## What this is
`LEXXII-Materialize-Forever` is Interactive-only. Sleep, lid, VIDEOIDLE, and When sharing media can already be honest. A theme still rotates every 10–30 minutes. GPU/DWM ticks. `powercfg /requests` may show a DISPLAY holder during a change. The operator thinks Dim display after came back. The slideshow fired. On a plugged-in Grok desk the pass is **Paused** (slideshow not available on AC). On battery may stay Available if the laptop actually travels.

This is not Sleep. This is not VIDEOIDLE. This is not VIDEODIM. A black panel with a handshake is an off timer. A nits drop after idle minutes is Dim display after. A wallpaper change on a live, enumerated desktop is this row.

Grok in-session schedulers expire in 7 days even when durable. Recreate them if missing. Host-forever is Task Scheduler `LEXXII-Materialize-Forever`. Not a Linux FULL AUTO daemon.

## What to change (plugged-in Grok desk)
1. Confirm display idle and sharing layers are already done — VIDEOIDLE 0, VIDEODIM 0 or skip, When sharing media Allow the computer to sleep. See [turn-off-display-after](turn-off-display-after.html) and [multimedia-sharing-sleep](multimedia-sharing-sleep.html).
2. Unhide Desktop background settings → Slide show if ATTRIB_HIDE. Missing before this step is expected on many Windows 11 images.
3. Power Options → Change plan settings → Change advanced power settings → **Desktop background settings** → **Slide show**. Set **Plugged in** to **Paused**. On battery may stay Available if the laptop actually travels.
4. Leave When sharing media, When playing video, and display timers alone on this click.
5. Leave the current plan in place. Not a switch to High performance. Do not Apply all on Energy recommendations.
6. Confirm. Do not paste live brokerage numbers. Do not paste `auth.json`.
