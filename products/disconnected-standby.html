Published 2026-09-01 · 824 Consultants LLC · Windows Grok host playbook

This is not Connected Standby / CSEnabled and not Network connectivity in Standby. CSEnabled asks whether this class can leave Modern Standby and get S3 back. Network-standby is the NIC policy during Connected Standby. This page is the remaining flavor: powercfg /a reports Standby (S0 Low Power Idle) Network Connected vs Network Disconnected. Disconnected Standby is still S0ix. It is not S3. On this Yoga the DWORD is missing. Do not invent DisconnectedStandbyMode=1.

The problem

Do not mint a Vercel slug because powercfg /a said Network Connected.

What to change
