Reserve battery level vs a forever loop

Published 2026-09-01 · 824 Consultants LLC · Windows Grok host playbook

This is not Low battery action and not Critical battery action. Those are dump verbs. This page is the leftover percent: Power Options → Battery → Reserve battery level. BATRESERVELEVEL. GUID f3c5027d-cd16-4930-aa6b-90db844a8f00. Default is often 7%. Operators cargo-cult 0 thinking it disarms Low/Critical. 0 is not never. Reserve is not a dump.

Download markdown Low battery first Critical battery

The problem

Low and Critical can already be Do nothing on a docked desk. The tray still shows a reserve band. Someone sets Reserve to 0 to “turn off battery lies.” That is not a pass. Reserve is the emergency floor. It does not Sleep the session. A lying gauge that jumps to 5% is still Critical or Low. Do not raise Reserve to 20 as a fake UPS. Do not install systemd.

What to change

  1. Confirm Low and Critical are already Do nothing on this docked desk (or skip if missing).
  2. Query BATRESERVELEVEL. OEM 7 is a skip, not a fail.
  3. Do not set 0. Leave the percent.
  4. Leave notification toasts alone. A toast is not a dump.
powercfg /query SCHEME_CURRENT SUB_BATTERY BATRESERVELEVEL

Soak test

  1. Low and Critical already Do nothing.
  2. Reserve still OEM. Session does not Sleep because of this row.
  3. Task Scheduler still has a next-run for LEXXII-Materialize-Forever. A surprise Sleep is a verb fail, not a reserve fail. Grok schedulers expire in 7 days even when durable. Host-forever is that named Windows task. Not systemd.
  4. Missing GUID = skip. No fourth Grok TUI.
