Fighting-for vs liquid

Published U.S. unclaimed-property pool is on the order of $80 billion, with roughly $3 billion returned in a year and about a 1-in-10 search hit. Typical hits are often $100–$5,000. That pool is fighting-for money. It is not 824 Consultants cash on hand. Liquid here stays $0 until a receipt.

What this is not

Not a church or charity vacuum of heirless escheat. Heirless property goes to the state. Cy pres is court-gated leftover, not NAUPA.

Not instant power of attorney over a stranger. You claim as owner or lawful heir with ID on the official door.

Not a paid finder. Official search is free. Finder letters that demand a cut first are a known scam pattern.

Do not paste Social Security numbers, dates of birth, or match results in comments or on this site.
