Windows 11 Energy saver slider vs a forever loop

Published 2026-08-31 · 824 Consultants LLC · Windows Grok host playbook

This is not the legacy Battery saver page, not Processor power throttling (EcoQoS), and not the Power mode overlay. A docked AC Grok desk should not sit under Energy saver. Windows 11 24H2 put a live control in Quick Settings (Win+A) and an auto-on battery threshold under Settings → System → Power & battery → Energy saver. Microsoft copy tells people to leave Energy saver on all the time. That overlay dims ~30%, pauses background work, greys Power mode, and makes an Interactive Grok CLI look hung while Task Scheduler still prints Ready and last result 267009 (still-running). The named task did not die. The slider was on.

The problem

Timeouts can already be honest. Lid close on AC can already be Do nothing. Minimum processor state can already be 100. Boost mode can already be Enabled. The brick is in the wall. The chassis is on a dock. The TUI still starves. The leaf sits on the battery / power icon. That leaf is the Windows 11 Energy saver slider, live this session.

Two controls, one overlay. The operator-facing slider is Quick Settings → Energy saver. Tap it on a docked desk and the overlay is live even though AC is in. The other half is Settings → Turn energy saver on automatically when battery level is at (Never / 10% / 20% / 30% default / 40% / 50% / Always). A USB-C dock that reports On battery, or Plugged in, not charging, lets that auto threshold fire while the GaN brick is still in the strip. Default 30% is a travel value. Always in that dropdown is a fail on a desk that never unplugs. grok.exe stays alive. Background tool calls pause. The operator opens a fourth TUI. Wrong move.

This is not sleep. powercfg /a still names the same standby type. Requests can still be None. Processor power throttling can still be Off and EcoQoS can still be a different liar. Legacy Battery saver (pre-24H2, DC-only) is a different name and a different page. Always use energy saver (On / Adaptive) is a different Settings row — if that row is On, this auto-on control is often hidden. Do not install systemd to paper over a leaf. Do not mint a Vercel slug because the TUI paused. Do not switch the whole plan to High performance to hide a Quick Settings tile.

What to change (docked AC Grok desk)

  1. Confirm the timeout layer is already done — plugged-in sleep / hibernate are not 5–20 minutes. Lid close on AC is Do nothing. Fast Startup is already honest. See sleep-vs-forever and lid-close. If the desk still intends to sleep every idle, stop here.
  2. Confirm Minimum processor state is already honest on AC — 100 on plugged-in. If the floor is still 5%, that is min-state, not this page. Energy saver on top of a 5% floor is two liars.
  3. Confirm this host says Energy saver, not Battery saver — 24H2 and later. If Settings still labels the card Battery saver, this is the wrong page. Legacy Battery saver was DC-only. Do not cargo-cult a 24H2 slider onto a 23H2 name.
  4. Confirm Windows reports Plugged in while docked — Settings → System → Power & battery, top of the page. On battery with the brick in the wall is a dock / PD / cable lie, not a slider bug. Honest e-marked cable, GaN brick that actually negotiates, dock firmware. The auto-on threshold fires on whatever Windows thinks is DC. Do not hide a 15 W charger by tapping Energy saver Off.
  5. Live slider Off now — Win+A (or the battery / power icon) → Energy saver. Off. The leaf over the battery means the overlay is live this session. A docked desk that never unplugs does not leave the tile on “until the next plug.” Scroll the Quick Settings grid if the tile is hidden on 24H2. Power mode must become changeable again after this tap. If it stays greyed, the overlay is still on.
  6. Auto-on threshold → Never — Settings → System → Power & battery → Energy saver → Turn energy saver on automatically when battery level is at → Never. 10 / 20 / 30 / 40 / 50 are travel values. Always in that dropdown is a fail on a desk that lives on a dock. Leave a travel cube at 30% only if it actually leaves the wall. This is not the Always use energy saver row. If that Always-use row is On or Adaptive, the auto-on control is often hidden — different layer, stop, do not guess.
  7. Leave Lower screen brightness when using energy saver alone — that checkbox only matters when Energy saver is actually on (travel DC). Unchecking it does not disable the CPU cap. Do not treat a brightness box as the docked pass.
  8. Leave Power mode alone on this page — Best power efficiency / Balanced / Best performance is a sibling overlay. If that row is greyed, the Energy saver slider is still on. Do not cargo-cult Best performance to hide a leaf.
  9. Leave Processor power throttling alone on this page — EcoQoS / execution speed is not this tile. Energy saver Off with background work still starved is a different playbook. Do not paste a power-throttling registry hack here.
  10. Leave the Balanced (or current) plan in place — this page is the live slider plus the auto-on threshold, not a switch to High performance. Do not rename the plan. Do not claim a fleet GPO. Do not Apply all on Energy recommendations.
  11. Confirm, then soak — query the energy-saver subgroup. The plan can already look honest while the Quick Settings tile is still On. Settings and Win+A are the clicks. Do not paste live brokerage numbers next to that output. Do not paste auth.json.
powercfg /query SCHEME_CURRENT SUB_ENERGYSAVER ESBATTTHRESHOLD
powercfg /query SCHEME_CURRENT SUB_ENERGYSAVER ESBRIGHTNESS
powercfg /a

ESBATTTHRESHOLD is the plan’s auto-on battery percentage. Typical travel default is 30 (0x0000001e). That query does not prove the live Quick Settings slider is Off, and it does not prove Settings Never on every build — Never is a Settings click, not a public powercfg /energy-saver-off. Do not write /setdcvalueindex to 0 and claim Never; 0 is not documented the same way on every 24H2 servicing stack. ESBRIGHTNESS is the dim scale, not the on/off. powercfg /a still names the standby type; this page is not sleep. If SUB_ENERGYSAVER or ESBATTTHRESHOLD is missing, this firmware or edition did not expose the old Battery-saver subgroup — stop; do not invent a registry hack on a public page. Hidden is not Off. Quick Settings leaf gone + auto-on Never + Power mode still changeable + Settings showing Plugged in is the pass for a docked desk. Do not paste a GUID pastebin. Do not paste auth.json.

How it splits from the other playbooks

  • Sleep vs forever — Modern Standby, Interactive-only logon, result 267009 still-running as a sleep tell. This page is an awake overlay.
  • Minimum processor state — plan frequency floor (PROCTHROTTLEMIN). Energy saver can starve on top of 100.
  • Processor performance boost mode — plan turbo allow (PERFBOOSTMODE). Not the leaf.
  • Legacy Battery saver (battery-saver-ac-desk) — pre-24H2 name, DC-only. If the card still says Battery saver, that page, not this slider.
  • Processor power throttling — EcoQoS / execution speed on background work. Survives with Energy saver Off. Not this tile.
  • Always use energy saver — Settings overlay On / Off / Adaptive. Different row. Often hides the auto-on control while it is On. Not this click.
  • Power mode — Settings → System → Power & battery → Power mode (Best power efficiency / Balanced / Best performance). Sibling overlay. Energy saver greys that row while the slider is on.
  • Energy recommendations — Apply all can shorten sleep and display timers. Wrong card. Can undo sleep-vs-forever.
  • USB-C PD honesty / GaN brick / dock — hardware that makes Windows report AC. This page assumes AC is already true.
  • This page — live Energy saver slider Off and auto-on Never on a docked AC Grok desk so 24H2 cannot cap CPU, dim, and pause an Interactive Grok CLI while the named task still looks Ready.

Soak test (what "passes" means)

  1. Timeouts already honest. Lid already Do nothing on AC. powercfg /requests already None. Minimum processor state already 100 on AC. Otherwise this soak is lying.
  2. Settings → Power & battery shows Plugged in (not On battery, not a surprise Plugged in, not charging). Dock and brick still seated.
  3. Quick Settings Energy saver is Off. No leaf on the battery / power icon. Power mode is still changeable (not greyed). Screen brightness matches the operator’s set value, not a surprise 30% cut.
  4. Auto-on is Never on a desk that never unplugs. Always and 30% with a lying dock are fails on this page.
  5. Leave the desk idle 30 minutes, lid closed, cooling stand in place, dedicated NIC plugged. The Grok TUI still accepts input. A session that paints but never completes a tool call because Energy saver paused background work is a fail on this page.
  6. Task Scheduler still has a next-run for LEXXII-Materialize-Forever. Ready + 267009 with the leaf on is not forever. Host-forever on this Windows profile is that named task. Grok in-session schedulers still expire in 7 days even when durable — recreate them if missing. Do not claim the Windows task is a Linux systemd unit.
  7. No fourth Grok TUI. Last result 267009 is still-running, not proof the slider is already Off.
