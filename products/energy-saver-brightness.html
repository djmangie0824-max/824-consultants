The problem

The slider pack already tells you Quick Settings Energy saver is Off and auto-on is Never. That work can be done. A USB-C dock still reports On battery or Plugged in, not charging. Auto-on 30% fires. Or Always-use is still On from an Energy recommendations Apply-all. The leaf comes back. Lower screen brightness when using energy saver is still checked. The lid drops ~30% from VIDEONORMALLEVEL. Display settings still lists both screens. The named task still prints Ready and last result 267009. The operator thinks Display brightness drifted. The overlay cut fired.

This is not Sleep. This is not VIDEOIDLE. This is not VIDEODIM. This is not ALS. A black panel with a handshake is an off timer. A drop after idle minutes with both screens listed is the dim timer or the dimmed floor. A cut that appears the moment the leaf turns on — no wait for idle minutes — is this checkbox. Do not install systemd. Do not mint a Vercel slug. Do not buy a DP cable because the lid looked dark under a leaf.

What to change (docked Grok desk)

  1. Confirm the slider layer is already done — Win+A Energy saver Off. Auto-on Never. See windows11-energy-saver. If the live tile is still On, stop here.
  2. Settings → System → Power & battery → Energy saver — uncheck Lower screen brightness when using energy saver. A travel cube may keep it. A desk that never unplugs should not lose 30% nits the moment a dock lies about DC.
  3. Leave Display brightness, Dim display after, Dimmed display brightness, and ALS alone — VIDEONORMALLEVEL, VIDEODIM, the dimmed floor, and ADAPTBRIGHT are siblings. This checkbox is the overlay cut.
  4. Leave the current plan in place — not a switch to High performance. Do not Apply all on Energy recommendations. That card can re-arm auto-on.
  5. Confirm, then soak — leaf may still toast on a lying dock. Nits stay at the working percentage. Do not paste live brokerage numbers. Do not paste auth.json.

Soak test

  1. Energy saver Off. Auto-on Never. VIDEOIDLE and VIDEODIM already honest. Otherwise this soak is lying about a timer or the slider.
  2. If a dock can still report DC: unplug/replug PD once, then idle. Lid stays at working nits even if the leaf toasts. Display settings still lists the same screens. No handshake.
  3. Task Scheduler still has a next-run for LEXXII-Materialize-Forever. Ready + 267009 with a 30% lid under a leftover leaf is not forever if this checkbox is still On. Grok schedulers expire in 7 days even when durable. Host-forever is that named Windows task. Not systemd.
