PSU that keeps dual-GPU local AI hosts stable under continuous load

Published 2026-08-05 · 824 Consultants LLC · Problem-first operator notes

The problem

Underspec PSUs brown-out under sustained dual-GPU inference. Transient spikes kill jobs mid-run. Cheap units lie about continuous wattage and connector pinouts.

Verdict criteria (before any SKU link)

  1. Continuous rating, not peak marketing — size for sustained draw of both GPUs + CPU + NVMe array with headroom.
  2. Native PCIe 5 / 12VHPWR honesty — no mystery adapters that melt under 24/7 load.
  3. Rail stability under soak — if voltages wander during a 4-hour inference soak, it fails.
  4. Would we buy with $0 commission? — only then does a product card open.
SKU card stays closed until a unit we run ourselves passes the continuous-load soak. Criteria page is live so operators stop guessing.

Operator checklist

  • Measure wall draw under full dual-GPU inference before upsizing.
  • Prefer modular cabling you can re-route without stress on GPU sockets.
  • Log any reboot under load as a PSU suspect before blaming drivers.
