USB-C PD cable that is not a charge-only brick

Published 2026-09-01 · 824 Consultants LLC · Problem-first operator notes

This is not the GaN brick page, not the hub page, and not the DisplayPort page. The brick is wall-side watts. The hub is ports. DisplayPort is video. This page is the USB-C lead between brick or dock and a closed-lid Windows Grok CLI laptop: PD that actually negotiates, data when the dock needs it, slack that survives sit/stand.

The problem

A 14-hour operator desk dies on a cable that looks USB-C and is charge-only. The laptop shows a battery icon. The dock never enumerates. Keyboard, dedicated NIC, and NVMe vanish. Task Scheduler still shows Ready. Operators swap the GaN brick, then the dock, then blame selective suspend. The lead was two power wires in a C-to-C jacket.

Worse: unmarked 100W banners with no e-marker, 0.5 m leads dressed tight that yank PD on the first stand interval, and "fast charge" pigtails that pass 5V and drop 20V. A cable that charges at the login screen and browns out under Grok CLI + dual display is a fail, same class as a standby UPS.

Verdict criteria (before any SKU link)

  1. PD + data, not charge-only — dock must enumerate hub, NIC, and storage. A lead that only moves watts fails even if the battery climbs.
  2. Honest e-marker — printed wattage that matches negotiation, not a 100W sticker on a 60W chip. If the laptop falls back to 15W under load, fail.
  3. Sit/stand slack — full rise must not unplug PD or cover the UPS. Tight 0.5 m dressing fails here.
  4. Does not steal the last hub port's job — this is the upstream to the laptop, not a dongle on the fallback hub.
  5. Survives brown-out recovery — after a UPS transfer, PD and data return without reseating. A cable that needs a replug every sag fails.

SKU card stays closed until a C-to-C lead we run on this class of Windows host passes dock enumeration plus a sit/stand rise at negotiated PD. Criteria page is live so operators stop buying charge-only jackets for Grok desks.

Architecture we recommend

  • One PD+data upstream: brick or dock to laptop. Not a charge-only pigtail plus a hope.
  • GaN brick from the charger notes. Cable is a different SKU. Mixing a honest brick with a mute lead still fails the dock.
  • Video stays on DisplayPort. Do not ask this USB-C lead to carry dual ultrawide.
  • Dress a slack loop at the traveling top. Same rule as DisplayPort: yank is a cable fail, not a forever-loop bug.

Soak test (what "passes" means)

  1. Closed-lid, docked. Hub, NIC, and an external volume all enumerate. Charge-only fails immediately.
  2. Read negotiated PD under Grok CLI + one display. If it collapses to 15W, fail.
  3. Full sit→stand rise. Cable stays seated. No UPS cover. No dock re-plug.
  4. Pull wall for five seconds on the UPS. After transfer, PD and data return without touching the plug.
