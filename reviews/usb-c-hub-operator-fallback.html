USB-C hub that works when the dock is over-subscribed

Published 2026-08-05 · 824 Consultants LLC · Problem-first operator notes

The problem

Full docks claim 10 ports and drop half under dual display + NVMe enclosure + webcam. Operators lose keyboards mid-command or external SSDs disconnect during model copies.

Verdict criteria

  1. Upstream honesty — what chipset, what real bandwidth after display share.
  2. Power delivery without brown-outs — bus-powered vs wall-powered for external NVMe.
  3. Firmware path — hubs that never update and randomly disconnect fail.
  4. Would rebuy at $0 commission — repairability and replacement cost matter more than port count.
SKU pending. Use this page as the fallback architecture note: dock for displays, dedicated hub for storage + input when bandwidth fights.

Architecture we recommend

  • Primary dock: displays + PD to the host.
  • Secondary powered hub: keyboard, external NVMe, capture devices.
  • Never daisy-chain mystery hubs behind an already-saturated dock.
