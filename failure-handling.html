Operator steps

  1. Open the failed Actions run and read the failed job log.
  2. Note the stage: validate, assemble, upload, deploy, or checkout.
  3. validate: restore FULL AUTO strings and immersive hub title — never gut them to pass.
  4. upload/deploy/checkout: click Re-run failed jobs first (often transient).
  5. Confirm DEPLOY_STAMP.json shows a new SHA.
  6. Confirm hub still says Touch. Talk. Go. and autonomy still declares full auto posture.

Hard rules

  • Never gut FULL AUTO / zero-human crawl language
  • Never disable the deploy workflow to hide errors
  • Never publish private vault, keys, or trading secrets
